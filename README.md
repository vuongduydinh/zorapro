# ZoraPro

> Desktop app that automates sales and customer care on Zalo.

**Website:** [zorapro.thesoloos.com](https://zorapro.thesoloos.com)

ZoraPro is a desktop application for Windows and macOS that helps sellers reach prospects and look after customers on Zalo at scale, across multiple accounts.

## Who it's for

- Individual sellers and small shops in Vietnam
- Businesses that sell and support customers on Zalo

## Key features

- **Bulk personalized messaging**
- **Automated friend requests**
- **Group member scanning**
- **Contact tagging** to organize your audience
- **Campaign tracking** across multiple Zalo accounts
- Available for **Windows** and **macOS**
